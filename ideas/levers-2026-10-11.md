# Levers, 2026-10-11

Bigger directions for Jamming, beyond polish. Written after the presets round (PR #6: 23 artists, each with its own figures). Each lever gets an impact and effort rating (H/M/L), and **cheap experiments** are marked 🧪. Several overlap ideas in `improvements-rejected.md`. Those were rejected as round-sized polish, so where a lever would revive one, it says so and the call stays with Stephen.

## Where it stands

- The engine writes a full 32-bar take locally in 1–7 ms, in 8 styles × 23 artists × 50 tunes, with no model at all. That's the moat: the AI is optional colour on top of a band that already plays.
- Two AI modes: **Composer** (a director plans the chart, players write parts, best-of-N with a critic) and **Improviser** (players trade phrases live while the take plays).
- Sharing is a link that re-generates the take from settings and seed. Takes are saved per browser.
- No accounts, no backend state, no audio export.

## Cost per take (estimated from the code and the price table in `src/ai/models.ts`)

Defaults: director **Sonnet 5.5** ($2 in / $10 out per million tokens), players **Haiku 4.5** ($1 / $5).

| Take | Calls | Tokens (in / out) | Cost at list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Composer, 16 bars, Haiku for both roles, best of 1 (measured, round 4) | 3 | 7.6k / 1.5k | ≈ $0.015 |
| Composer, 16 bars, defaults (Sonnet directs), best of 1 | 3 | ≈ 8k / 2k | ≈ $0.03 |
| Composer, 32 bars, defaults, best of 4 | ≈ 10 | ≈ 35k / 9k | ≈ $0.12 |
| Improviser, 16 bars, defaults (measured: four jams cost ≈ $0.40) | many small | ≈ 64k / 4.5k | ≈ $0.10 |
| Improviser, 32 bars | | ≈ 130k / 9k | ≈ $0.20 |

The Improviser is input-heavy: every phrase call re-sends the chart and the band's recent bars. That's where prompt caching or a smaller window would pay off. **A cheaper default:** GPT-6 Luna lists at $0.10 / $0.50, so a 16-bar jam would cost about $0.01 on it, 10–20× cheaper. Haiku as director cuts a Composer take roughly in half. Neither has been judged for quality yet (see A3).

| A3 | **A judge loop that listens to features, not text.** Score takes on measured things (clash rate, motif recurrence, register spread, density arc), which the engine can already compute, and only ask the model for taste. | H | M | Makes best-of-N cheaper and less noisy. | A4 | **Cache the Improviser's context.** The chart and band description don't change during a jam: prompt-cache them, and send only the last 4 bars. | M | L | Input is about 93% of Improviser tokens, so this is the biggest single cost cut. |

## Risks

- **Rights and likeness.** Artist names on presets are descriptive ("play like"), with no samples and no melodies. Keep it that way: no copyrighted melodies, no artist photos, no "official"-sounding claims. A gallery (P2) and clips (P3) raise the stakes, so add a line that these are homages.
- **Cost blowups.** A public free tier (G4) with the Improviser at today's defaults could cost $0.20 per 32-bar take. Ship A2 and A4 first, and put a hard daily cap on the hosted key.
- **Scope drift.** Multiplayer (P5) and mic input (P6) are each a product's worth of work. Don't start both.
- **Taste regressions.** The signatures are measured (clash rates, figures detected in the notes) but nobody with ears has signed them off. A listening pass on the 13 clips in `jamming-artifacts/presets2/` should come before promoting them.
- **Rejected-list tension.** P3, P4, P7 and A5 brush against rejected items. Rejections were about round-sized polish; as product bets they need an explicit yes.

## Top 8

1. **P1 · Decide who it's for**: one survey, and it reorders everything else. 🧪
2. **A5 · AI as arranger**: one cheap call turns a sentence into a full jam; the engine plays every note. 🧪
3. **P3 + V3 · Clips and share cards**: the main way Jamming travels outside the app.
4. **A4 + A2 · Cache the Improviser and a cheaper default**: cuts a jam from about $0.10 to about $0.01, which makes a free tier possible. 🧪
5. **P2 · Take gallery**: social proof and a reason to come back, cheap because takes are seeds.
6. **M5 · Artist blends**: signatures are already separable, so this is mostly UI for a lot of novelty. 🧪
7. **A3 · Feature-based judge**: better best-of-N for less money, using metrics the engine already computes.
8. **P6 → M3 · Play along with MIDI, then call and response**: the deepest lever. Start with MIDI on a blues.
